### 15. The Contraception-AMR Correlation: Assessment

**Does contraception access correlate with AMR rates at the country level?** YES — ecological correlation exists.

**Is it causal?** PROBABLY NOT in the direct pathway (pill → fewer births → less AMR).

**What drives both?** Economic development, governance quality, healthcare infrastructure, education, and institutional trust.

**Key confounders:**
1. GDP per capita (MAJOR)
2. Healthcare system quality (MAJOR)
3. Education levels (MAJOR)
4. Governance/regulatory capacity (MAJOR)
5. Agricultural antibiotic use (MODERATE)
6. Religious/cultural conservatism (MODERATE)

**Counter-examples:**
- Israel: TFR 3.0, moderate AMR, strong stewardship
- Japan: TFR 1.2, RISING AMR in elderly
- India: declining fertility, RISING AMR (OTC sales, pharma pollution)

**Defensible claim:** The SAME governance failures that restrict contraception also enable antibiotic misuse. The correlation is real and meaningful, but the mechanism is institutional, not biological.
