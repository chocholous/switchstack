The Confounder The contraception-AMR correlation is real. But economic development drives both. Israel (TFR 3.0, moderate AMR) and Japan (TFR 1.2, rising AMR in elderly) both complicate the simple narrative. The mechanism is institutional: good governance produces both good reproductive health AND good antibiotic stewardship. The same failures that restrict contraception also enable antibiotic misuse.

The data doesn’t care about your morals. Countries that trust women with their own bodies also trust science with their medicine. The correlation is institutional, not biological. And it kills in both directions.
