The Invisible Legacy: What Stays Behind
🌊

Kakhovka Dam

June 2023. Landmines, sewage, heavy metals, pesticides flushed into the Black Sea. Marine habitats buried under contaminated mud.

🌾

Poisoned Breadbasket

Every shell impregnates soil with lead, cadmium, mercury. 100–300 years to clean naturally. Ukraine's choice: remediate or eat the metals.

🧬

Nanoplastics

Drone casings, uniform fibers pulverized into particles that bypass biological barriers, infiltrating soil microbiome and water tables.

💨

Toxic Dust

Soviet-era buildings release asbestos and carcinogenic concrete dust when turned to rubble. Respiratory disease guaranteed for those who clear debris.
