## CO₂ / Atrocity Ratio Analysis

### Methodology
- CO₂ figures from peer-reviewed sources (Queen Mary/SSRN for Gaza, IGAW for Ukraine)
- Casualty figures from official/verified sources (OCHA, OHCHR, CSIS estimates)
- Oct 7 ground assault CO₂ is editorial estimate (see above)
- "Killed" includes combatants and civilians unless noted
- Small arms casualties NOT separated (data unavailable), but vast majority of deaths in both conflicts are from explosive/CO₂-intensive weapons (airstrikes 63% in Gaza, drones 70-80% in Ukraine)

### The Table

| Actor | CO₂ | Killed | t CO₂ per person killed |
|-------|-----|--------|------------------------|
| **Gaza — IDF + US (first 60 days)** | ~280,000 t | ~70,000+ (full conflict) | ~4 |
| **Gaza — Hamas rockets (full conflict)** | 713 t | ~15 | ~47.5 |
| **Gaza — Oct 7 ground assault** | ~40 t (estimate) | ~1,200 | ~0.03 |
| **Ukraine — Russia (3 years)** | ~154 Mt | ~325,000+ military + ~10,000 civilians | ~460 |
| **Ukraine — defense (3 years)** | ~41 Mt | ~55,000+ own military killed | ~745* |

*Ukraine defense ratio uses own military killed, not enemy killed — reflects CO₂ cost of defensive warfare per own soldier lost. If measured against Russian military killed (~325k), the ratio would be ~126 t/kill.

### Key Insights

1. **Oct 7 ground assault (0.03 t/kill)**: Three orders of magnitude below everything else. Maximum human devastation with near-zero carbon footprint. CO₂ metrics fundamentally cannot capture the scale of atrocity.

2. **Hamas rockets (47.5 t/kill)**: Paradoxically the most carbon-inefficient weapon per casualty. Iron Dome makes them militarily futile while their CO₂ cost remains. Each rocket that gets intercepted is pure waste — carbon emitted for zero military effect.

3. **IDF "precision" operations (4 t/kill)**: Low ratio, but the absolute scale is staggering — 70,000+ killed, 123,000+ buildings destroyed, up to 61 Mt long-term CO₂. "Precision" does not mean "sustainable."

4. **Russia in Ukraine (460 t/kill)**: Industrial-scale warfare. Hundreds of tonnes of CO₂ for every person killed. Massive, grinding destruction.

5. **Ukraine defense (745 t/kill own losses)**: Highest ratio — defensive operations (fortifications, logistics, counter-strikes) are carbon-intensive relative to own losses. If measured as CO₂ per enemy killed (~126 t), it's lower than Russia's ratio, suggesting more efficient use of resources.

### What This Shows
The CO₂/atrocity ratio reveals that carbon footprint and human suffering are **decoupled**. The deadliest single event (Oct 7) had the smallest footprint. The most carbon-intensive operations (Russia's campaign) are not necessarily the deadliest per tonne. "Precision" and "sustainability" in warfare are marketing terms — the data shows no correlation between technological sophistication and reduced human cost.
