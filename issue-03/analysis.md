## Corrections and Caveats

### Critical Corrections
1. **1.35M --> 1.19M**: The commonly cited "1.35 million road deaths/year" is from 2018 data. The current WHO figure is 1.19 million (2021 data). Articles must use 1.19M.
2. **Motorcycle externalized risk**: Per-km, motorcycles impose HIGHER third-party risk than cars (7.63 vs 3.25 ORU deaths/billion vkm). The narrative must acknowledge this. The "switch" is about absolute numbers and framing, not per-km third-party risk.
3. **Speed survival ranges**: Different studies give different numbers. Use ranges (5-13% at 30 km/h, 29-40% at 50 km/h) or cite specific study.

### [UNVERIFIED] Claims
- "Average European car weighs 1,800 kg" -- actual average is ~1,521 kg (2021) to ~1,947 kg (2023 new-car test average). The 1,800 kg figure is plausible as a current fleet average but not specifically sourced.
- "Average SUV weighs 2,200 kg" -- average SUV/crossover tested 2023 = 1,906 kg. New models reaching 2,600+ kg. The 2,200 kg figure is in the range but high for current average.
