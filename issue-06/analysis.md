### Oregon Measure 110 (2021–2024)

| Event / Metric | Details | Source |
|----------------|---------|--------|

## The Drug Ledger: Death Toll Comparison

| Substance | Legal Status (US) | Annual US Deaths | Schedule | Source |
|-----------|------------------|-----------------|----------|--------|
| Tobacco | Legal (21+) | 480,000 | Unscheduled | CDC |
| Alcohol | Legal (21+) | 178,000 | Unscheduled | NIAAA |
| Prescription opioids | Legal (Rx) | 14,716 | II–IV | CDC 2023 |
| Synthetic opioids / fentanyl | Legal (Rx) + Illegal (IMF) | 72,776 | II | NIDA 2023 |
| Heroin | Illegal | 5,871 | I | NIDA 2023 |
| Cocaine | Illegal | 29,449 | II | NIDA 2023 |
| Cannabis | Illegal (federal) | 0 (direct overdose) | I | CDC/DEA |
| Psilocybin | Illegal | 0 (known) | I | No documented fatal OD |
| MDMA | Illegal | Rare (hyperthermia, water intoxication) | I | — |

**The Switch:** The three deadliest substances (tobacco, alcohol, prescription opioids) are all legal. The two Schedule I substances with the lowest death tolls (cannabis, psilocybin) are classified as having "no accepted medical use" — despite growing clinical evidence for psilocybin and state-level medical/recreational cannabis programs.
