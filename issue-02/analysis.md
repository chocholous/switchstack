## Corrections and Flagged Items

### Items Requiring [PARTIALLY VERIFIED] Tag in Articles

1. **40,000 children figure**: Based on UNICEF estimates from ~2012-2014, repeated in Amnesty 2016. No updated independent census. Flag: [PARTIALLY VERIFIED -- based on 2012 UNICEF estimates, no updated census available]

2. **2 million liters water per tonne lithium**: Upper bound from older estimates. Current peer-reviewed data (2024) gives 500,000-600,000 liters direct brine consumption. Flag: [PARTIALLY VERIFIED -- figure represents total lifecycle water footprint; direct brine consumption is lower]

3. **<5% battery recycling rate**: Actual rate is likely 5-10%. >90% of batteries are still landfilled. Flag: [PARTIALLY VERIFIED -- exact global rate uncertain; commonly cited range is 5-10%]

### Items That Are VERIFIED

- 78% of cobalt from DRC (2024)
- Children as young as 6-7 in mines
- $1-2/day wages
- 82% of miners with skin problems (RAID 2024)
- 85.4% with breathing issues (RAID 2024)
- 52.7% with major injuries in past year
- Cobalt lawsuit dismissed March 2024
- US DOL designation expanded September 2024
- Kalando mine collapse: 32+ dead (November 2025)
- Tesla at 49% on Lead the Charge (top score)
- EU Battery Regulation traceability mandate (2027)
